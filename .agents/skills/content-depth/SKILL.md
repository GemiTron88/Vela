# Skill — Content Depth & Localization

1. Discover and audit content surfaces with `executionkit content status .`, `executionkit content discover .`, and `executionkit content validate . --boundary <task|wave|phase|release>`.
2. Run `executionkit guard lang .` to verify translation key parity and locale completeness across all supported languages (including RTL/LTR).
3. Eliminate thin placeholder copy, duplicate boilerplate, and untranslated strings across public pages, transactional emails, and empty/error states.
