# SEO authority rule

When SEO is applicable, load `docs/capabilities/seo-os.md` and the project `executionkit.seo.json` before making search-architecture decisions.

- `TODO.md` remains the sole mutable backlog authority.
- SEO contracts/inventories/evidence are declarative or derived, never shadow task state.
- Do not apply SEO scoring to private/authenticated/API/native-only surfaces.
- Route/database existence does not imply index eligibility.
- Programmatic indexability is fail-closed until distinct intent/value/coverage are proven.
- Structured data must reflect truthful applicable visible facts.
- Do not claim guaranteed rankings, indexing, traffic or rich results.
- Content/Test/Audit/Launch/State authority boundaries remain intact.
